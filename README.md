# Chromatic Symbiosis 🌌

> *"To unlock the portal to a new world, a father must guide his sons through the mystery of light."*

An animated, interactive 2.5D web experience bringing a personal Photoshop movie-style poster to life. Built with **Next.js**, **Framer Motion**, and **Tailwind CSS**, featuring multi-plane parallax depth, bioluminescent light waves, and an interactive portal gateway leading to the artwork's story and making-of.

---

## 📖 The Story: Life Beneath the Ice of Saturn

Set on a rocky, ice-crusted ocean moon of Saturn sealed beneath a thick frozen ceiling and violent stormy atmosphere:
- **Light as Life:** Without sunlight or photosynthesis, all living beings generate their own bioluminescent light and internal heat. 
- **Chromocommunication:** Instead of spoken words or human sight, this intelligent alien species communicates, navigates, and solves problems exclusively through **projected colored light waves and thermoreceptive heat sensation**.
- **The Discovery:** While exploring an unvisited underwater mountain cave, twin brothers discover three warm, floating organic forms (autumn leaves, an impossible phenomenon in their world).
- **The Living Portal:** As their father guides and protects them, the twins join hands to wrap their light around the leaves, unlocking a glowing compass that activates the giant swirling purple tree-like entity—a living dimensional portal.

---

## 🎨 Creative Inspiration & Craft

- **Family Inspiration:** Created by a father of young twin boys. A real conversation with his son about the colors of fallen autumn leaves inspired this sci-fi family adventure.
- **100% Manual Photoshop Compositing:** Crafted by hand in Adobe Photoshop without generative AI—meticulously balancing layer masks, color harmony, differentiating the twin silhouettes, and integrating real autumn leaf photography into glowing light streams.

---

## ✨ Web Features

- **2.5D Multi-Plane Parallax:** Reactive spring-damped parallax responding to mouse cursor and mobile gyroscope motion across 8 distinct layers.
- **Tree Portal Gateway:** An interactive hotspot button embedded in the cosmic tree vortex with dimensional warp transitions to the `/story` page.
- **The Story & Making-Of (`/story`):**
  - *Chapter 1: The Narrative Lore* (Saturn's frozen ocean moon, thermoreceptive alien family).
  - *Chapter 2: The Father's Journey & Inspiration* (Real-life twin sons, fallen autumn leaves).
  - *Chapter 3: The Craft* (Manual Photoshop compositing techniques & interactive 8-layer dissection viewer).
- **Atmospheric Smoke & Bioluminescent Particles:** Continuous screen-blended mist drift and Canvas-based prismatic light embers.
- **Exploded 3D Layer Inspector:** Toggle 3D perspective to pull apart and inspect each individual Photoshop layer in 3D space.
- **Ambient Soundscape:** Subtle underwater cosmic drone with interactive light chime frequencies.
